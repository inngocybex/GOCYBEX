About GOCYBEX

Building Sustainable Cybersecurity Capability

GOCYBEX is an Integrated Cybersecurity Capability and Transformation Partner — helping organizations strengthen people, processes, technology, governance, and resilience through education, advisory, engineering, talent, and research.

Our Story

Every successful organization begins with a purpose that extends beyond the products it builds or the services it delivers. At GOCYBEX, that purpose is rooted in a simple but powerful belief: organizations become secure not merely by deploying technology, but by building sustainable cybersecurity capability.

GOCYBEX was established to address this challenge through a fundamentally different perspective — not as another cybersecurity vendor, but as a capability-building organization dedicated to helping institutions strengthen their long-term ability to prevent, respond to, recover from, and continuously adapt to evolving cyber threats.

Cybersecurity should not be viewed as a one-time project or compliance obligation. It should become an integral organizational capability that enables resilience, business continuity, innovation, and digital trust.

Vision, Mission & Purpose

The foundational statements that guide every decision, partnership, and engagement at GOCYBEX.

Our Vision

To become a globally trusted cybersecurity capability-building organization that empowers academia, industry, government, and society to build resilient, secure, and digitally trusted organizations.

Our Mission

To enable organizations to build sustainable cybersecurity capability by strengthening people, processes, technology, governance, and resilience through integrated education, advisory, engineering, talent, and research solutions.

Our Purpose

Every organization should be able to operate with confidence in an increasingly digital world. GOCYBEX exists to help make that possible — strengthening the capabilities that enable organizations to anticipate, prevent, respond to, and recover from evolving cybersecurity challenges.

Meet Our Founder

The vision and expertise behind GOCYBEX's cybersecurity capability-building mission.

SS

Dr. Shubhamangala Sunil

Founder & CEO, GOCYBEX
  • M.Tech, Computer Science
  • B.Tech, Computer Science
  • Bachelor in Business Administration
  • PG in Cyber Law
  • Certified Anti‑Terrorism Specialist

Dr. Shubhamangala Sunil is a highly seasoned information security professional and a leading authority in cybersecurity within India, with over two decades of experience spearheading information security projects and services across SMEs, large corporations, and multinational organizations.

Her expertise extends to engagements with public sector agencies and government departments across India, Australia, the USA, the Middle East, and South-East Asia — including counsel provided to government security agencies such as the Supreme Court of India, the Indian Navy, and NTPC. She is a member of the National Cyber Safety and Security Standards Technical Committee, and her multifaceted skill set spans technical training, curriculum design, cybersecurity auditing, digital forensics, cyber policy formulation, and cyber law consultancy.

A frequent speaker at industry platforms including APWG, STC (USA), NASSCOM, ASSOCHAM, and FKCCI, Dr. Sunil has also shaped cybersecurity education by developing curricula for institutions such as the National Law School of India University, and has outlined skill outcomes for M.Tech programs at universities in India and abroad. She founded GOCYBEX to translate this institutional and government-facing experience into a sustainable, capability-building partnership model for organizations worldwide.
